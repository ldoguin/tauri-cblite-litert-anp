What this demonstrates

It keeps working offline, and nothing is trusted blindly

  • Offline first (shown). With the link down the tablet still wrote its report; the review request waited in the replicated inbox and went out by itself.
  • Every document is signed by the device that wrote it, and checked against that device’s DID before anyone acts on it (the badges on each pane).
  • Scoped by token. A device can only message the agents its token names; a revoked or foreign token is refused at the door and logged.
  • Sealed end to end. The task text is encrypted to the device it is for; a relay only ever holds ciphertext.
  • No cloud. Three Couchbase Lite databases, replicating peer to peer.
Refusals, revocation and the encryption claims are checked by the test suite, not shown above. Reproduce it: scripts/record-anp-video.ps1  ·  run the checks: cargo xtask all
