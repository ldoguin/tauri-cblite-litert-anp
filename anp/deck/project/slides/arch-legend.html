Red outlines are Couchbase products. With no gateway, a store replicates straight to the head office’s Couchbase Lite, over TLS with DID admission.

Links 1 to 4 are the Agent Network Protocol and cross company boundaries. Links 5 to 8 are Couchbase replication inside the retailer. Link 9 is how the authority’s Biscuit tokens and revocations reach the stores: through the head office, which carries them in as documents. The authority’s public key is also given to every company, so it can verify a store’s token offline.
