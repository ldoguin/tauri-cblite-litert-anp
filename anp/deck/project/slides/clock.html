What the store sees

Then The Clock Starts

01:50

Freezer 1 sticks in defrost with its heaters on, and the dairy chiller’s compressor stops.

02:40

Fifty minutes later the ice cream and frozen vegetables, €9,200, are past their safety limit. Then freezer 2 fails too.

02:41

€17,800 of frozen meat and fish has about 45 minutes left. €8,600 of dairy has about 112.

Food-safety rules, not the model, decide what is lost. What is past the limit is never moved or given away.

The fault is in the cold room, not the network, so what follows works with the store’s internet link down. The assessment runs the cooling model forward: how many minutes each product has left, ranked by urgency and value. A limit with an allowance in minutes, and a hard limit past which nothing is kept.
