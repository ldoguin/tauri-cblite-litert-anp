Head Office Sees Every Store

Three stores, their failing units, their agents’ phase and what they have spent.

Never asked. Head office asks no store anything: it reads documents it has verified.

Store 0412 is done. Stores 0207 and 0933 wait for their duty managers.

The head office shows every store whose signed records it holds: each store’s units with their temperature and fault, its rescue agent’s phase, and what it has spent, worst first. The data is only the replicated temperature logs and rescue records, so it needs nothing from a store but the replication link.
