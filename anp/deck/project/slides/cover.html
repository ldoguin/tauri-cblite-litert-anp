Agent Network Protocol on Couchbase Lite

Cold-Stock Rescue

A store’s own agent saves what it can, with companies it has never worked with.

A simulation: the store, the companies and their prices are fictional. The protocol, the signatures and the encryption are real.

Opening. The night after the October 2026 attacks on French supermarkets: a store’s freezers fail overnight, and its own agent finds a truck, cold storage, a food bank and an insurer, commits money only within a limit the retailer signed, asks a person when the plan costs more, and leaves a record anyone can check. Couchbase Lite holds the store’s signed records; Sync Gateway and Couchbase Server carry them to head office.
