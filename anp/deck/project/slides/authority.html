A holder can narrow a token, never widen it. Every company checks it itself.

The store’s authority to spend is a Biscuit token bound to its DID: €6,000 per commitment, and the budget caps the total. Past either, the agent cannot sign: a person decides. Raising the budget is a person’s decision; a higher cap would need a new token from the retailer’s authority.
