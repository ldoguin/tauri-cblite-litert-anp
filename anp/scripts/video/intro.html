Agent Network Protocol on Couchbase Lite

Field inspection that survives no signal

A technician in a basement. Three devices, no cloud: a field tablet, a branch office and head office, each its own database, joined by peer-to-peer replication.

  • Identity. Every device is a did:wba identity (ANP-03), derived from its own key.
  • Trust. Tokens signed by one authority and bound to each DID: the tablet may message the branch and head office only.
  • Privacy. Task text is end-to-end encrypted, links are TLS with a pinned certificate, databases are encrypted at rest.
  • Discovery. Each device publishes a signed agent description (ANP-07); peers verify it with no call to anyone’s domain.
In this recording a rule-based agent stands in for the tablet’s on-device model; everything else is the real thing.
