What made it possible

The agent reasoned freely, but could only sign what its token allowed.

  • Found and verified strangers: DIDs on each company's own domain, signed messages, no shared platform.
  • Spent within limits the other side enforced: a Biscuit token bound to the store's DID, checked by every seller.
  • Kept a record no one can rewrite: €10,500 committed in six deals, each four signed messages that audit offline.
  • Coordinated in private: an MLS group whose host relays what it cannot read.
  • Seen from head office, inside the retailer: every store's signed records replicate to it, peer to peer or through Couchbase Sync Gateway; the authority's revocations and new tokens flow back the same way.
