Agent Network Protocol on Couchbase Lite

Cold-stock rescue: agents across companies

In October 2026, attackers put hundreds of supermarkets' refrigeration controllers into defrost overnight. This is the night after: a store's own agent saves what it can, with companies it has never worked with.

  • Strangers, found and checked. Three carriers, two warehouses, a food bank, an insurer and a supplier, each a did:wba on its own HTTPS origin, found through a search agent.
  • Limited, delegated authority. The store may commit up to €6,000 per order. Every company checks that itself.
  • Private and provable. End-to-end encrypted conversations and groups; every quote, acceptance and confirmation is signed.
  • A person above the limit. Over its budget, the agent can only ask.
A simulation: the store, the companies and their prices are fictional, and the agent plans by rules. The protocol, the signatures and the encryption are the real thing.
